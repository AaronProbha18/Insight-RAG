# Lumen Ridge Trail E-Bike Owner Manual

The Lumen Ridge Trail E-Bike is a hardtail electric mountain bike designed for forest trails and gravel roads. This manual covers model year 2026.

## Specifications

| Specification | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Motor | 250 W rear hub motor |
| Battery | 500 Wh removable lithium-ion |
| Weight | 23.4 kg including battery |
| Maximum rider weight | 120 kg |
| Assisted top speed | 25 km/h |
| Frame sizes | S, M, L |

## Range

Range depends on the assist mode. In Eco mode the bike travels up to 60 km on a full charge. In Trail mode the range is up to 45 km. In Boost mode the range is up to 30 km. Cold weather below 5 degrees Celsius can reduce range by up to 20 percent.

## Charging

Use only the supplied 4 A charger. A full charge from empty takes about 5 hours; charging from 20 percent to 80 percent takes about 2 hours.

- Charge the battery at temperatures between 10 and 30 degrees Celsius.
- Do not leave the battery on the charger for more than 24 hours.
- For storage longer than one month, keep the battery at around 60 percent charge.

## Display Error Codes

| Code | Meaning | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E01 | Motor overheated | Stop riding and let the motor cool for 15 minutes |
| E04 | Battery not detected | Remove and reinsert the battery |
| E07 | Speed sensor fault | Check the magnet on the rear spoke |

## Maintenance

Have the bike serviced every 1,000 km or every 6 months, whichever comes first. A service includes brake pad inspection, chain lubrication, and a firmware check.

Brake pads should be replaced when the pad material is thinner than 1.5 mm. Keep tyre pressure between 1.8 and 2.2 bar for trail riding.

## Warranty

The frame has a 3-year warranty. The battery has a 2-year warranty, or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first. Damage from crashes or unauthorised modifications is not covered.
