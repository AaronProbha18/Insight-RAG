# Leadership Meeting Notes, Q3 2026

Meeting held on 8 July 2026. Attendees: the Head of Retail, the Head of Product, the Head of People Operations, and the Finance Lead.

## Decisions

### Trail E-Bike Launch

The Trail E-Bike launch date was moved from 1 September to 15 September 2026, because the battery supplier delayed the second shipment. Pre-orders open on 1 August 2026.

### New Store

The leadership team approved opening a fourth store in the harbour district. The target opening date is 1 November 2026. The Head of Retail owns the fit-out budget of 180,000.

<!-- pagebreak -->

### Returns Policy Review

The team discussed extending the general return window from 14 to 30 days. No change was made; the team will revisit the question in Q1 2027 after reviewing return-rate data.
