# Lumen Ridge Outfitters Returns Policy

This policy applies to all purchases made in Lumen Ridge Outfitters stores and on the Lumen Ridge website. It was last revised on 3 March 2026 and replaces the 2024 version.

## General Return Window

Most items can be returned within 14 days of the delivery date for a full refund. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by the order number or receipt.

Returns that arrive after 14 days but within 21 days are accepted for store credit only. Returns after 21 days are not accepted, except under the warranty terms below.

## Footwear

Footwear has a longer window: boots and trail shoes can be returned within 30 days of delivery, even if they have been worn indoors. Footwear with visible outdoor wear, such as mud on the soles, is not eligible for return.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## Exceptions

The following items cannot be returned:

- Gift cards
- Fuel canisters and other hazardous goods
- Personalised or engraved items
- Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout

Opened water filters can be returned only if they are faulty.

## Refund Methods

| Purchase method | Refund method | Processing time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Card in store | Same card | 3 to 5 business days |
| Card online | Same card | 5 to 7 business days |
| Cash | Cash or store credit | Immediate |
| Gift card | Store credit | Immediate |

Original shipping costs are not refunded, unless the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## Warranty Claims

Tents, backpacks and sleeping bags carry a 2-year manufacturing defect warranty. The Trail E-Bike carries a separate 3-year frame warranty and a 2-year battery warranty, described in its manual.

To make a warranty claim, contact the Customer Care Team with the order number and photos of the defect. The Customer Care Team responds within 2 business days.
