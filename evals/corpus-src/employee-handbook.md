# Lumen Ridge Outfitters Employee Handbook

This handbook applies to all employees of Lumen Ridge Outfitters. Questions about it go to the People Operations Team.

## Working Hours

### Store Staff

Store staff work rostered shifts of up to 8 hours. Rosters are published at least 14 days in advance. Shift swaps must be approved by the Store Manager.

### Office Staff

Office staff have core hours from 10:00 to 15:00. Outside core hours, start and finish times are flexible. Office staff may work remotely up to 2 days per week.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## Leave

### Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.

### Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

### Adventure Days

Every employee receives 2 Adventure Days per year to spend outdoors. Adventure Days cannot be carried over and must be booked at least 7 days in advance.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## Expenses

### Travel

Rail travel is preferred for journeys under 4 hours. Mileage for personal cars is reimbursed at 0.30 per km.

### Meals

The daily meal allowance during business travel is 40. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

### Submitting Claims

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense, with receipts attached. Claims above 500 need approval from a Department Head.

## Staff Discount

Employees receive a 30 percent discount on full-price items and 10 percent on sale items. The discount does not apply to gift cards.
